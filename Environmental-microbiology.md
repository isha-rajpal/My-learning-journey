# Environmental Microbiology 🌱

## Introduction
Environmental microbiology focuses on the role of microorganisms in natural environments.

## Key Concepts
- Microbes play a role in nutrient cycling  
- They help in biodegradation of pollutants  
- Used in wastewater treatment  

## Example
Microbial consortia can be used to remove dyes from industrial effluents.

## Importance
- Helps in maintaining ecological balance  
- Useful in bioremediation
